Founded in Reading, MA in 1985 and relocated to Malden in 1999 at the invitation of former Mayor Richard C. Howard — to support the city's growing Asian immigrant population and serve as a liaison between Asian and mainstream communities across Greater Boston.

Why Support CCC?

For over 40 years, CCC has been the cultural bridge between Asian immigrant communities and the broader public. Your support sustains free programs, amplifies underrepresented voices, and keeps Greater Boston's diverse communities deeply connected.
