Who We Are

A bridge between American and Chinese communities — built to last.

Founded in 1985 and rooted in Malden since 1999, the Chinese Culture Connection helps Chinese immigrants and their children appreciate and retain their heritage, develop bicultural fluency, and share the richness of Chinese language and culture with everyone around them.

"Through education, dialogue, and celebration, we help neighbors of every background understand one another — and belong."

Trusted stewardship

A registered 501(c)(3) public charity serving the Greater Boston area. Every gift is tax-deductible and reinvested directly into programs for children, families, and seniors.

Signature Events

Celebrations that bring the whole region together.

From a Lunar New Year gala that fills a high-school auditorium to a Thanksgiving tournament where six-year-olds rally against eighty-six-year-olds, our events turn culture into community.

Flagship Celebration

The Malden Lunar New Year Celebration Gala

Now in its sixteenth year, our flagship gala welcomes 1,000 to 2,000 guests for an evening of lion dance, music, food, and tradition — one of the largest Lunar New Year celebrations north of Boston, and a homecoming for families across the region.

春節

Annual Fundraising Gala & Awards

21st Year · Every Spring

An elegant evening honoring community leaders and raising the support that keeps our programs free and affordable for the families who need them most.

Thanksgiving Ping Pong Tournament

11th Year · 200+ Athletes

Fifty teams and players aged 6 to 86 gather every Thanksgiving for friendly competition that has become a beloved Greater Boston tradition.

AAPI Heritage Celebration

Every May

Honoring the untold stories of Asian immigrants and their journey to America, with performances, food, and family activities open to all.

East Meets West Dialogue

Monthly Series

A monthly cross-cultural conversation — from Chinese Yue opera meeting Western opera to Tang poetry traveling across four languages.

Arts & Culture Workshops

Year-Round

Hands-on workshops — masks around the world, Chinese knot-making, calligraphy, and more — that put living traditions into children's hands.

Malden Resident Meetup

Third Monday Monthly

A warm, regular gathering where neighbors share snacks, games, and resources — and where new immigrant families find a first friendly door.

Our Story

Four decades, one mission.

1985

A classroom idea

Founded in Reading, MA, when a mother created an "Intro to Chinese Culture" curriculum for her son's class.

1999

Home in Malden

Relocated to Malden to serve its fast-growing Asian community and connect it with the city's diverse neighborhoods.

2025

A new beginning

Opened a 1,600 sq ft headquarters at 75 Pleasant Street — room at last for classes, meetings, and growth.

Today

Forty and growing

Serving thousands each year through education, the arts, and community programs across Greater Boston.
