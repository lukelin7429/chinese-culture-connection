A note for parents

It looks like after-school care. It's really where children grow bicultural roots.

Many families think we simply watch the children until pick-up. Today's showcase tells the truer story: in these hours, kids fall in love with their language, their heritage, and the courage to stand in front of a crowd.

On Stage Today

One afternoon. Six ways a year of learning came alive.

No child sat this one out. Each took part — and each performance was the visible tip of skills built quietly, week after week.
