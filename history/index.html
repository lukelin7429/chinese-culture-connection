Our Story · 1985–2025

Four Decades of Building Bridges我們的故事

For 40 years, the Chinese Culture Connection has grown from one parent's classroom idea into one of Greater Boston's most enduring bridges between Chinese immigrant communities and the broader public.

A 40-Year Journey
1985

A classroom idea

Founder Catherine Hsu creates an “Introduction to Chinese Culture” curriculum for her son's class in Reading, MA. The warm response grows it into a nonprofit dedicated to sharing Chinese language and culture.

1999

A home in Malden

At the invitation of then-Mayor Richard C. Howard, CCC relocates to Malden to support the city's fast-growing Asian immigrant population and serve as a liaison between Asian and mainstream communities across Greater Boston.

2000s
–2010s

Traditions take root

Signature celebrations are born — the Malden Lunar New Year Celebration (now one of Greater Boston's largest free cultural events), the Thanksgiving Ping Pong Tournament, the Annual Gala & Awards, East Meets West dialogues, and the Little Dragon youth programs.

2025

A new chapter, in our 40th year

CCC opens a new headquarters at 75 Pleasant Street, launches the bilingual CCC Quarterly Journal, and pilots three new programs — a Community Bilingual Library, Bilingual Rhythm Time, and Friday Tai Chi.

Today

Serving thousands every year

Through education, the arts, and community across Massachusetts, New Hampshire, and Rhode Island — and just getting started.

How it began

It started with one mother wanting her son's classmates to understand and celebrate Chinese culture. Forty years later, that simple act of sharing has touched thousands of families — newcomers and neighbors alike.

“Where cross-cultural understanding begins.”
Our Mission

Through diverse educational programs, cross-cultural dialogues, and special events, CCC empowers Chinese immigrants and Chinese descendants of Greater Boston to appreciate and retain their heritage, develop bicultural fluency, and enrich others with a deeper understanding of Chinese language and culture.

Leadership

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded by Catherine Hsu and led today by Executive Director Mei Hung, guided by a dedicated volunteer Board of Directors.
